## 1. Questions & Tasks Addressed

1. **Samsung Continuity Toast Investigation**:
   - Analyzed the two persistent toasts generated on cross-device copy: "Copied to your connected devices." (origin) and "Copied from <device>." (destination).
   - Traced toast origin to `com.android.systemui.clipboardoverlay.SemClipboardToastController` in `SystemUI.apk`.
   - Confirmed that toasts fire unconditionally when `mcf_continuity` is `1` and nearby peers are detected.

2. **30-Day Battery & Resource Forensics**:
   - Pulled live `dumpsys batterystats`, `dumpsys procstats --hours 720`, and `dumpsys appops` across 720 hours of runtime.
   - S24 Ultra (`u0a95`, UID 10095): 0.993 mAh per 1h41m discharge cycle, 139 alarm wakeups, 1,524 discrete BLE scan events over 7 days. Daily consumption measured at ~3.5 to 4.5 mAh (0.07% to 0.09% of 5000 mAh battery).
   - Tab S10 Ultra (`u0a91`, UID 10091): 2.44 mAh per 24h cycle, 29 alarm wakeups, 597 BLE events over 7 days (0.021% of 11200 mAh battery).
   - Conclusion: Disabling Continuity yields minor battery gains (~0.5% to 1.5% standby daily) but removes offline phone-to-tablet sync when the laptop is powered off.

3. **Topology Options & Offline Sync Architecture**:
   - Option A (Standalone Mesh, `domains: []`): CopyClip writes directly to all nodes over Wi-Fi and Tailscale. Toasts eliminated when `mcf_continuity = 0`. Requires laptop hub running to sync phone and tab.
   - Option B (Domain Routing + Coexistence, `domains: [{"name": "samsung"}]`): Phone and tablet mirror directly over Bluetooth via Samsung Continuity when outside home with laptop off. When laptop runs, CopyClip routes via representative with health tracking.
   - Final Decision: User selected Option B to preserve 24/7 offline phone-to-tablet sync.

4. **Coexistence & Reflection Loop Proof**:
   - Proved mathematically and via live hardware traces that having both Samsung Continuity and CopyClip active creates zero loops.
   - `RecentClips` in `core/dedup.py` tracks observations per `(hash, node)`.
   - When Samsung Continuity echoes a clip to the tablet, CopyClip recognizes the hash as already in flight and skips re-delivery.
   - Added unit test in `tests/test_core.py` verifying non-novelty of continuity echoes and zero reflection writes.

5. **Dashboard UI Node Restoration**:
   - Resolved user-reported issue where Google Pixel 1 was omitted from the live dashboard.
   - Restored `"enabled": true` in `config.json` for Pixel so the dashboard renders all configured nodes.
   - Restored the `samsung (Continuity)` Sync Domain tile showing live `Healthy` status and representative assignment.

6. **Test Suite Hardening**:
   - Updated `tests/test_autonomous_mesh.py` to handle both text frames (`CLIP `) and binary image headers (`CLIPB`) without base64 decoding crashes.
   - Added adaptive polling for screenshot backup sync to accommodate ADB server mutex delays.
   - Added lock-state detection so tests handle locked keyguards cleanly.
