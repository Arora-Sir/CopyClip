## 1. Questions & Tasks Addressed

### 1. Reverse Direction Direct TCP Streaming (Windows to Android)
* **Problem**: Even on a high-speed local network (150 Mbps link), sending large images from Windows to Android previously required 11 to 18 seconds through ADB stdout/stdin chunking.
* **Root Causes**:
  1. ADB stdin/stdout pipes perform character-level stream serialization and base64 parsing overhead.
  2. High-level socket readers (`BufferedReader`) read ahead into internal 8 KB buffers, which breaks byte alignment during transitions from ASCII command parsing to raw binary byte ingestion.
* **Implementation**:
  1. `android/ClipBridge.java` binds an inbound `ServerSocket` on an ephemeral port via `initInboundStreamServer()`.
  2. The bridge announces its port to Windows over standard output via `STREAM-PORT <port>`.
  3. `nodes/android_node.py` records `_stream_port` and executes `_try_direct_stream()` on image delivery.
  4. Mutual HMAC-SHA256 authentication: Android sends `STREAM-AUTH <nonce>`, Windows replies `STREAM-RESP <hmac>`, Android confirms with `STREAM-OK`.
  5. Windows sends `STREAM-IN <sha256> <bytes>` followed by raw binary payload.
  6. `readAsciiLine(InputStream in)` reads headers byte-by-byte up to `\n`, avoiding read-ahead byte corruption.
  7. If direct TCP connection is unavailable or times out after 3.0 seconds, the bridge falls back cleanly to ADB chunking.

### 2. Lossless WebP Transcoding Analysis
* **Question**: Will Lossless WebP transcoding for camera photos preserve full photo fidelity without blurring?
* **Analysis**:
  1. WebP Lossless (VP8L specification) is mathematically exact. The spatial transform and color indexing reconstruct identical RGB pixel values with zero compression artifacting.
  2. Camera photos from modern mobile devices (such as Galaxy S24 Ultra) carry critical proprietary metadata: Samsung Extended Format (SEF), embedded motion photo video clips, dynamic range HDR gain maps, and manufacturer Exif.
  3. Transcoding `.jpg` or `.heic` into `.webp` strips these auxiliary metadata streams even when pixel arrays remain exact.
* **Decision**: Pass through original `.jpg` and `.heic` bytes unmodified to preserve all camera features and metadata intact.

### 3. Per-Device Camera Subfolder Organization
* **Requirement**: Provide an optional setting to segregate camera photos by source device to prevent filename collisions.
* **Implementation**:
  1. Added `"camera_device_subfolders": false` to `config.json`.
  2. Updated `nodes/windows_node.py` in `_save_camera_photo()` to route files into `Temporary Screenshots/Camera/<device_name>/` when enabled.
  3. Wired setting through `core/hub.py`, `copyclip.py`, and `windows_app/dashboard.py` with live GUI checkbox.

### 4. Pixel 1 Offline Connection Logging Dampening
* **Requirement**: Avoid log spamming when Pixel 1 is kept powered off for months at a time, while guaranteeing immediate reconnection once powered on.
* **Implementation**:
  1. In `nodes/android_node.py` (`_supervise()`), track consecutive failure counts.
  2. After three consecutive connection failures, demote logs from WARNING to DEBUG.
  3. Emit an INFO heartbeat log only once every 30 minutes to verify the background supervisor is running.
  4. Relax retry backoff progressively up to 120 seconds to minimize idle CPU overhead.

### 5. Future Tracker Backlog Additions
* **Documented in `PROJECT_TRACKER.md`**:
  1. System tray icon context menu toggles for Camera Auto-Backup and Screenshot Auto-Saving with runtime hot-reload.
  2. Complete elimination of Windows modal dialogs (`messagebox.showinfo` / `messagebox.showerror`) and console window flashes in favor of live in-app status indicators.
